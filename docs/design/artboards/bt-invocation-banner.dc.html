Marketplace item page: invocation banner

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-25 (A99). It replaces the bordered info box that was built first. The banner is a gradient panel with no border and 16 px corners. Inside are centred pills in the composer's surface colour, each with the item's tag, the prompt and an arrow, then a centred footer line. Items with no listed colour take a first stop from banner.palette.1-8 (Ruling R-IB1).

Panel

  • No border, no shadow, radius.2xl (16 px), padding 24 px.
  • Linear gradient at 135 degrees: the first stop is at the top left, the second at the bottom right.
  • With two listed colours, the gradient runs from the first to the second. With one, it runs from that colour to banner.end. With none, it runs from banner.palette.N to banner.end.
  • Listed colours are mixed toward surface.canvas until text.primary reaches 4.5:1 on them.

Pills

  • Centred column, up to 560 px wide, with 8 px between pills.
  • Each pill is 44 px high, with 8 px corners, filled with surface.raised (the composer's surface) and no border.
  • Inside: the tag chip (item mark and name, badge.neutral.bg), the prompt in body on one line ending in an ellipsis, and the arrow-right icon in text.secondary.
  • Hover uses fill.hover.raised. Each pill is a tab stop. Pressing one starts a chat with its full prompt.

Footer and palette

  • 12 px below the pills, centred, body-sm, text.primary: "Type @Tag in any chat or room to ask it directly."
  • The palette slot is FNV-1a of the extension ID, modulo 8, plus 1, so an item gets the same colour on every run.
  • Every palette stop and banner.end give text.primary at least 4.5:1. The new tokens are not in tokens.json yet.
